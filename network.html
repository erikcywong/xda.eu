Service desk

One intake, three workflows.

Warranty, spares and technical issues enter through one form and route to the correct team by request type.

Raise a service request

Demo form. No ticket is created and no message is sent.

Route by request type: warranty → after-sales lead; spares → parts desk; technical → engineering. Include model and serial to cut triage time.

Warranty claim

Frame, motor, battery and controller claims. Attach serial and proof of sale when the live system is connected.

Spare parts

Fast-moving items: brake pads, tubes, belts, chargers, sensors and display units.

Technical ticket

Diagnostic support, torque settings, firmware questions and escalation to engineering.
